Horological & Precision Transparency Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Mechanic Time Ox, Manhattan Watchmaking Atelier

1. General Horological Notice & Scope

The technical horology treatises, caliber specifications, chronometric rate tolerances, and artisanal movement photographs published across the Mechanic Time Ox digital platforms and at our flagship manufacture located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, are presented exclusively for luxury horological appreciation and connoisseur education. While our master watchmakers engineer calibers to the highest chronometric standards, Mechanic Time Ox notes that mechanical timepieces are kinetic micro-machines influenced by environmental and wearing variables.

2. Mechanical Chronometric Rate Tolerances & Magnetism

Mechanical chronometers regulated by Mechanic Time Ox are calibrated to rigorous observatory standards (±1 to ±3 seconds per day). However, individual daily rate performance may vary slightly based on ambient temperatures, positional gravity orientations during sleep, mainspring torque states, and strong electromagnetic field exposure from electronic appliances. While our free-sprung balances provide exceptional isochronism, mechanical watches do not offer the absolute atomic synchronization of quartz or satellite timekeeping.

3. Artisanal Hand-Finishing & Minor Visual Nuance

Every bridge bevel, black-polished surface, and rose engine guilloché dial created by Mechanic Time Ox is executed entirely by the hand of a master watchmaker using manual tools, gentian wood pegs, and antique lathes. Consequently, microscopic variations in anglage reflection lines, chamfer widths, and guilloché cuts are the natural signature of authentic artisanal craftsmanship rather than manufacturing defects. These nuanced markers distinguish true hand-finished haute horology from mass-produced computerized CNC components.

4. Water Resistance & Gasket Maintenance

Water resistance ratings specified for our timepieces (typically 30m to 100m depending on caliber architecture) apply when all crowns and pushers remain fully screwed down and sealed against case gaskets. Water resistance is not a permanent attribute, as rubber sealing gaskets naturally age over time and should be tested annually by our atelier before swimming. Wearing dress chronometers in hot saunas or chlorinated whirlpools is strictly discouraged due to thermal gasket expansion risks.

5. Independent Manufacture & Historical References

Mechanic Time Ox is an independent haute horology manufacture. Historical references to master horologists such as Abraham-Louis Breguet, John Harrison, or George Daniels are made strictly for historical context, educational commentary, and horological lineage appreciation. Mechanic Time Ox is an independent entity and is not directly affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by commercial watch brands that produce contemporary trademarked iterations of historical inventions.
