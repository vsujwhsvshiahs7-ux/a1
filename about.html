Core Horological Science

Deadbeat Escapements & Invar Pendulum Physics

Mass-produced quartz clocks rely on battery-driven stepper motors that lack soul and longevity. At TickClockVan, we honor mechanical clockmaking traditions established by 18th-century horologists like George Graham and John Harrison.

Every longcase grandfather clock and marine chronometer we restore features deadbeat anchor escapements, compensated invar pendulum rods, and hand-polished flame-blued steel hands that deliver steady, unyielding impulse torque.

1. Deadbeat Escapement

Recoil-free Graham anchor mechanism delivering constant impulse.

2. Invar Pendulums

Zero-expansion nickel-iron alloy rods for thermal stability.

3. Fusee Barrel Torque

Conical gear equalizer ensuring linear 8-day power reserve.

4. Mobile Clockmaker Van

On-demand estate laboratory equipped with gear lathes & beat scanners.
