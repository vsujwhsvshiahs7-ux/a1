Industrial Micro-Engineering

0.001mm Micro-Tolerances & DLC Coatings

Mass-produced quartz watches rely on plastic circuits that fail under pressure. At MechanicTimeox, we engineer indestructible mechanical timepieces machined from aerospace grade-5 titanium billets.

Every chronograph engine and skeleton tourbillon machine we forge features laser-poised balance wheels, Vickers 4,500 DLC coatings, and sapphire exhibition casebacks that deliver unyielding precision across extreme environments.

1. Titanium Micro-Milling

0.001mm tolerances carved directly from grade-5 titanium billets.

2. DLC Diamond Coating

Vickers 4,500 plasma vapor deposition for extreme scratch resistance.

3. Monopusher Column-Wheel

Tactile single-pusher chronograph engagement architecture.

4. Mobile Workshop Van

On-demand estate laboratory equipped with 3D movement scanners.
