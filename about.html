Core Horological Science

Flying Tourbillons & Column-Wheel Chronographs

Mass-produced quartz watches rely on battery circuits that lack soul and longevity. At TickClockVan, we honor mechanical watchmaking traditions established by legendary Swiss masters.

Every mechanical wristwatch and monopusher chronograph we restore features hand-polished anglage bevels, free-sprung inertia poising screws, and flame-blued hands that deliver steady, unyielding chronometric precision.

1. Flying Tourbillon

60-second titanium cage negating 6-position gravity errors.

2. Free-Sprung Balance

Gold poising screws for superior rate regulation stability.

3. Column-Wheel Engine

Tactile monopusher chronograph engagement mechanism.

4. Mobile Watchmaker Van

On-demand VIP estate laboratory equipped with 3D movement scanners.
