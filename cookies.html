Cookie Policy & Web Telemetry

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Mechanic Time Ox, Manhattan Watchmaking Atelier

1. Understanding Cookie Files & Digital Architecture

Cookies are compact, encrypted alphanumeric text files deposited onto your personal computer, smartphone, or tablet when you navigate the digital portals of Mechanic Time Ox. These secure files enable our online showcase to remember your customized viewing preferences, maintain active selections within our interactive caliber configurator, preserve high-resolution zoom states, and provide an effortless browsing experience across return visits. Rather than invasive surveillance scripts, cookies represent an industry-standard mechanism essential for delivering a reliable, secure, and tailored luxury digital experience.

2. Essential Technical Operational Cookies

Our digital infrastructure deploys foundational technical cookies strictly required to ensure core platform functionality, navigational integrity, and digital security. Without these essential cookies activated, our digital atelier cannot maintain secure bespoke commissioning requests, deliver responsive 3D movement configurator tools, or authenticate collector portal appointments. Because these specific data tokens are technically indispensable for delivering the baseline luxury digital services requested by your web browser, they are deployed automatically upon initial site access and cannot be disabled.

3. Analytical Telemetry & Performance Measurement

To continually elevate the performance of our online catalog, Mechanic Time Ox utilizes privacy-conscious analytical cookies and diagnostic performance monitors, including Google Tag telemetry (gtag.js). These sophisticated measurement tools gather aggregated, pseudonymized metrics detailing visitor navigation pathways, average reading times on our horology research treatises, and device rendering speeds across diverse international regions. This telemetry provides our technical engineers with actionable insights, allowing us to optimize loading latency, refine interface layouts, and ensure seamless mobile responsiveness.

4. Personalization & Collector Preference Memory

Preference cookies allow our digital storefront to remember your deliberate stylistic selections and customized horological settings across visits. For instance, these cookies retain your preferred precious metal displays, localized currency values, and previously calculated caliber configurations within our interactive tool. By preserving these aesthetic choices, our platform spares you the inconvenience of re-entering preferences each time you return to view our latest horology publications, creating a harmonious and seamless digital experience.

5. Third-Party Integration & Tag Management

Certain specialized services hosted on our platform utilize secure third-party scripts to facilitate high-definition macro imagery rendering, typography loading, and secure analytics measurement. Trusted global infrastructure providers may deposit auxiliary cookies through our site to monitor script performance, maintain cryptographic handshake security, and prevent distributed denial-of-service disruptions. We rigorously vet every third-party technology vendor to guarantee that all deposited tokens comply strictly with international data protection protocols and do not harvest unencrypted personal identities.

6. Managing & Configuring Cookie Preferences

Collectors possess complete autonomy to accept, customize, or decline non-essential cookies at any time through their personal web browser preferences. Most modern browsers—including Safari, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge—provide intuitive settings panels where you can inspect active cookies, delete existing stored tokens, or establish blanket blocking rules for third-party scripts. Please be aware that disabling performance or preference cookies may alter certain interactive functionalities, such as the real-time feedback generated by our bespoke caliber configurator.

7. Atelier Contact for Cookie Governance

Should you require further clarification regarding our technical cookie implementations or wish to submit an official inquiry to our systems administrator, please reach out to our team. Inquiries may be addressed to Mechanic Time Ox, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our technical support staff at +1-888-777-5845 during standard atelier hours. Our technical division will gladly provide additional details regarding individual script behaviors and assist you in configuring your browser preferences for optimal viewing comfort.
