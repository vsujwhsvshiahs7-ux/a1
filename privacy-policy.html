Privacy Policy & Horological Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Mechanic Time Ox, Manhattan Watchmaking Atelier

1. Introduction & Client Discretion Mandate

At Mechanic Time Ox, operating our haute horology manufacture and private watchmaking salon at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold collector confidentiality, timepiece commissioning records, and personal privacy to the highest standards of luxury discretion. We recognize that commissioning bespoke mechanical chronometers, scheduling movement viewings, or purchasing limited edition calibers involves sharing private contact details, custom engraving requests, and commercial transaction records. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact protocols we employ to collect, process, safeguard, and retain your data across our digital platforms and Manhattan atelier.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our manufacture, we collect essential client information including your full name, private email address, telephone number, bespoke timepiece specifications, custom guilloché engraving preferences, and transaction details recorded during private commissioning consultations. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive caliber configurator or explore our horology research journal, our web servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths via Google Tag manager. These technical metrics enable our digital software engineers to optimize platform performance and protect transaction security across international collectors.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate horological manufacturing and client service operations, including executing custom caliber commissions, coordinating movement assembly appointments, managing warranty records, issuing certificate of origin documentation, and delivering progress updates during multi-month hand-finishing cycles. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to exclusive timepiece exhibitions and collector salon gatherings. We maintain an absolute policy never to sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute collector identities or commissioning records to any third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital commissioning archives, movement serial records, and billing information entrusted to Mechanic Time Ox are stored on private cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and strict biometric access controls. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors, ensuring our manufacture never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Mechanic Time Ox extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to profile records, restrict analytics processing, or demand the permanent deletion of your customer archives from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our client concierge desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain timepiece ownership records and movement serial numbers indefinitely in our archival manufacture ledger, enabling seamless lifetime servicing, provenance verification, and authentication for future generations of collectors without compromising your personal privacy. If you choose to conclude your relationship with our atelier or request personal data expungement, non-essential personal identifiers are securely purged from active marketing registries within thirty business days, while anonymized mechanical movement serial records are preserved to protect your timepiece's historical provenance.

7. Atelier Contact for Privacy Governance

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our leadership team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Mechanic Time Ox, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard atelier hours. Our compliance committee will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard commercial timelines.
