2. Bespoke Timepiece Commissioning & Deposits

Because our mechanical chronometers and tourbillon calibers are crafted individually by master horologists requiring months of precision hand-anglage, gear cutting, and custom guilloché engine-turning, bespoke commissions require a non-refundable initial production deposit upon contract signing. The remaining balance becomes due upon final quality control inspection and chronometric timing certification prior to delivery. Delivery timelines represent master craftsman estimates subject to delicate hand-finishing standards, and clients receive documented photographic progress dossiers throughout assembly.

3. Five-Year Manufacture Warranty & Servicing

Mechanic Time Ox extends a comprehensive five-year international manufacture warranty covering any mechanical defects in movement components, gear train synchronization, or escapement functionality under normal wearing conditions. All warranty servicing and regular five-year overhauls must be performed exclusively by our certified watchmakers.
